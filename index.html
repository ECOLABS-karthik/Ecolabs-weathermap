Press for…
  • Check the weather & approx. energy generation: select a route first, then view the weather and estimated energy along it.
  • 🧭 Route (left side, ringed): plan a trip. Enter a starting point (or use your location) and a destination, pick the departure time, then press Show route.
  • ☰ Settings (right-side buttons, ringed): switch map layers on or off, and under Regions press a name (Europe, Africa, North America…) to load all the other places.
Reading your route
  • Legends: the Weather legend explains the map colours. The Route energy legend shows how much power each stretch of your route generates (the coloured line). Tap a coloured stretch for its details.
  • Data below: the bottom panel shows distance and travel time, then estimated energy from ☀️ solar, 💨 turbines and 🔥 heat (TEG), the total, and your approximate driving range. Scroll it for the full breakdown.
Choose a mode: Road, Train or Sea
  • ROADS: live weather over land, plus 🧭 Route for driving trips and their energy estimate.
  • TRACKS: shows the railway lines for train travel. Driving routes and satellite view are turned off here.
  • OCEANS: ocean weather and shipping routes. Use ⚓ Ship route to plan a trip by sea.
Switch between them with the ROADS / OCEANS / TRACKS tabs at the top of the map.
